# Crónicas del Crepúsculo Geriátrico - Parte 08

## End-of-Life Care

- [P0389] **OBLIGACIÓN** — VA policy requires interdisciplinary Palliative Care consult team at each VA facility
- [P0390] **HECHO** — Hospice and Palliative Care services = part of Veterans Health Administration Standard Medical Benefits Package
- [P0391] **PERMISO** — All enrolled Veterans eligible for hospice/palliative services if they meet clinical need
- [P0392] **PERMISO** — Veteran has choice between VA benefit or Medicare benefit to fund hospice services
- [P0393] **PERMISO** — No VA policy prevents Veterans from receiving hospice services in addition to other palliative care (e.g., whole brain irradiation, radiation for painful/bleeding metastatic disease, palliative chemotherapy/immunotherapy) if VA not funding duplicate care
- [P0394] **RESTRICCIÓN** — Dual model of hospice + palliative care subject to acceptance by community-based hospice

## Introduction

- [P0395] **HECHO** — Nursing facilities now admit medically complex patients discharged from acute care and specialty hospitals, beyond traditional custodial care
- [P0396] **HECHO** — Both LTC residents and post-acute patients can be medically complex with multi-morbidities and risk factors for geriatric syndromes (pressure injuries, anorexia, falls)
- [P0397] **DEFINICIÓN** — Persons living in nursing facilities = "residents"; those admitted for short-term medical/skilled care = "patients"
- [P0398] **HECHO** — LTC residents often need acute care in addition to ongoing chronic medical care
- [P0399] **HECHO** — Short-stay patients often have chronic conditions + need for acute and post-acute medical care; some may have extended stay or transition to LTC bed
- [P0400] **HECHO** — Attending physicians primarily responsible for managing individual patient medical care + facilitating IDT collaboration
- [P0401] **REGLA** — Medical director roles/functions are separate from attending physician roles, even when same person serves both
- [P0402] **DEFINICIÓN** — Attending physician provides direct resident care; medical director oversees, coordinates, helps improve facility's overall medical care/services

## The Role of the Medical Practitioner

- [P0403] **OBLIGACIÓN** — OBRA '87 required every NF resident/patient to have attending physician to supervise and coordinate medical care → `OBRA 1987`
- [P0404] **DEFINICIÓN** — "Supervising the care" = participating in assessment/management, monitoring changes in medical status, providing consultation/treatment when needed
- [P0405] **HECHO** — Supervising care includes performing regulatory visits and possibly supervising NPs or PAs
- [P0406] **PERMISO** — NPs currently permitted to serve as "attending practitioner" for hospice patients
- [P0407] **HECHO** — AMDA developed curriculum + certification program for medical directors and curriculum for attending physicians covering diverse competency areas
- [P0408] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Physician must approve resident admission (e.g., giving/approving orders upon admission) → `Table 1 OBRA'87`
- [P0409] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Physician must be familiar with and contribute to patient assessment/care planning → `Table 1 OBRA'87`
- [P0410] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Physician must take active role in supervising patients' care: aware of impact of others' medical actions, ensure coordination → `Table 1 OBRA'87`
- [P0411] **OBLIGACIÓN** — At each visit: review total program of care including medications/treatments by other disciplines → `Table 1 OBRA'87`
- [P0412] **OBLIGACIÓN** — At each visit: write, sign, date progress note → `Table 1 OBRA'87`
- [P0413] **OBLIGACIÓN** — At each visit: sign and date all orders (except immunization orders that may be periodic without new order) → `Table 1 OBRA'87`
- [P0414] **OBLIGACIÓN** — At each visit: evaluate resident condition + continued appropriateness of current medical regimen → `Table 1 OBRA'87`
- [P0415] **REGLA** — Visit frequency: ≥1× every 30 days for first 90 days after admission; ≥1× every 60 days thereafter → `Table 1 OBRA'87`
- [P0416] **REGLA** — Next scheduled regulatory visit date determined by admission date, not actual date of last visit → `Table 1 OBRA'87`
- [P0417] **REGLA** — Visit is timely if occurs ≤10 days after required date → `Table 1 OBRA'87`
- [P0418] **PERMISO** — Daily visits allowed but must be medically necessary → `Table 1 OBRA'87`
- [P0419] **REGLA** — After initial visit, required visits may alternate between attending physician visits and PA/NP/CNS visits under physician supervision → `Table 1 OBRA'87`
- [P0420] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Physician must respond appropriately and timely when notified of acute change of condition → `Table 1 OBRA'87`
- [P0421] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Physician must ensure backup medical coverage (individual physician, physician group, or APN) if attending unavailable; virtual visits may be allowable → `Table 1 OBRA'87`
- [P0422] **REGLA** — Physician may delegate tasks to PAs/NPs/CNSs consistent with OBRA'87 requirements and state licensure/scope of practice → `Table 1 OBRA'87`
- [P0423] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Physician must be aware of role of consultants and intervene when care inadequate, problematic, or not pertinent → `Table 1 OBRA'87`
- [P0424] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Practitioner must assess new admissions in timely fashion → `Table 2`
- [P0425] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Practitioner must sign monthly orders, interim orders, lab/test results; review facility consultant pharmacist recommendations → `Table 2`
- [P0426] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Practitioner must provide information/documentation to help staff determine appropriate level of care for new admission → `Table 2`
- [P0427] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Practitioner must provide pertinent discharge summary within 30 days of patient discharge or transfer from NF → `Table 2`
- [P0428] **OBLIGACIÓN** — If pending transfer to another practitioner, continue providing all necessary medical care until another physician takes over → `Table 2`
- [P0429] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Practitioner must provide pertinent progress notes covering condition (medical/psychiatric stability), current status, prognosis, goals → `Table 2`
- [P0430] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Practitioner must designate alternate coverage and inform facility about communication with practice/alternate coverage → `Table 2`
- [P0431] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Practitioner must notify facility of any extended absence and related coverage arrangements → `Table 2`
- [P0432] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Practitioner must define significance of symptoms/problems, clarify/verify diagnoses, help establish prognosis and realistic care goals → `Table 2`
- [P0433] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Practitioner must periodically review continued relevance of all prescribed medications; identify/address medication-related adverse consequences → `Table 2`
- [P0434] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Practitioner must guide ethics-related decisions (e.g., options for life-sustaining treatments) → `Table 2`
- [P0435] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Practitioner must document pertinent rationale for medical decisions consistent with clinical, legal, regulatory requirements → `Table 2`
- [P0436] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Practitioner must complete all physician information on death certificates in timely manner → `Table 2`
- [P0437] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Practitioner must be alert to observed/suspected violations of resident rights including abuse or neglect → `Table 2`

## Clinical Reasoning and Diagnostic Quality

- [P0438] **REGLA** — Key steps in patient-centered medical plan: (1) clarify relevant medical issues, (2) determine decision-making capacity, (3) identify primary/proxy/surrogate decision maker, (4) look at big picture, (5) review individual's values/wishes/preferences/goals, (6) reconcile patient goals of life and goals of medical care, (7) order appropriate interventions where benefit outweighs risk, (8) monitor and adjust interventions
- [P0439] **DEFINICIÓN** — Diagnostic quality = minimizing and recognizing diagnostic errors that contribute to undesirable outcomes
- [P0440] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Facilities and practitioners should review care processes and identify diagnostic/thinking errors that can result in inappropriate/harmful treatment

## The Role of the Medical Director

- [P0441] **HECHO** — Medical directors serve in various settings: hospitals, insurance companies, specialty programs (dialysis, hospice, wound care, PACE), some assisted living facilities
- [P0442] **DEFINICIÓN** — Medical director = "physician who oversees medical care and other designated care and services in a health care organization or facility" (federal regulations)
- [P0443] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Federal regulations require nursing facility medical director practice

## Medical Director Characteristics

- [P0444] **HECHO** — Most NF medical directors have internal medicine or family medicine background; ~1 in 4 also trained geriatricians → `Resnick et al. 2009`
- [P0445] **HECHO** — Medical director may cover 1 or several facilities
- [P0446] **HECHO** — Many medical directors also serve as attending physicians; significant number act solely as medical director with no attending responsibilities

## Origins of the Medical Director Role

- [P0447] **HECHO** — NF medical director role evolved from government investigations of 1970 salmonella outbreak in Maryland nursing facility
- [P0448] **HECHO** — 1970s: AMA Committee on Aging suggested medical director roles/functions and recommended physician education on basic roles/responsibilities
- [P0449] **REQUISITO** — By 1974, skilled nursing facilities (certified for Medicare skilled nursing services) required to retain full- or part-time medical director
- [P0450] **HECHO** — OBRA 1987 expanded medical director requirements to include residential + skilled portions of nursing facilities
- [P0451] **DEFINICIÓN** — For regulatory purposes both skilled and non-skilled facilities = "nursing facilities"
- [P0452] **HECHO** — Surveyor guidance (42 CFR 483.75(i) Medical Director [F501], updated as F841) clarified CMS expectations
- [P0453] **HECHO** — Institute of Medicine (2001) recommended NF medical directors be given greater authority; structures/processes developed to enable/support/require more focused physician participation

## Key Medical Director Responsibilities

- [P0454] **HECHO** — AMDA developed program to certify medical directors as having specific administrative/medical knowledge
- [P0455] **HECHO** — As of 2022, requirements for medical directors in assisted living facilities were minimal
- [P0456] **REGLA** — F-Tag 841 covers medical director roles/responsibilities: (1) implementation of resident care policies, (2) coordination of medical care in facility
- [P0457] **REGLA** — NF can be cited for deficiency if no medical director fulfilling requirements; most F-Tag 841 citations must relate to another F-Tag deficiency

## Regulatory Foundation

- [P0458] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Federal regulations require every NF in USA to retain physician as medical director
- [P0459] **HECHO** — Primary resource of medical director regulations = OBRA '87 + State Operations Manual on surveyor guidance; plus some individual state regulations
- [P0460] **DEFINICIÓN** — "F-Tags" = discrete segments of federal NF regulations related to ≥1 specific regulatory requirements used for State/Federal surveys
- [P0461] **DEFINICIÓN** — "Current standards of practice" = approaches to care, procedures, techniques, treatments based on research/expert consensus contained in current manuals, textbooks, publications, or accepted/adopted/promulgated by recognized professional organizations/national accrediting bodies → `State Operations Manual`
- [P0462] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Facility expected to obtain medical director input into all clinical policies/procedures including services by other health care disciplines

## Medical Director Responsibilities (F-Tag 841 — Table 3)

- [P0463] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Medical director must be knowledgeable about current professional standards of practice in LTC and how to coordinate/oversee other practitioners → `F-Tag 841`
- [P0464] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Medical director must help facility develop process to review basic physician/practitioner credentials (licensure, pertinent background) → `F-Tag 841`
- [P0465] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Medical director must help facility ensure residents have adequate primary attending and backup physician coverage → `F-Tag 841`
- [P0466] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Medical director must have meaningful input into development, review, approval, implementation of resident care policies → `F-Tag 841`
- [P0467] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Medical director must support/promote person-directed care: advance directives, end-of-life care, provisions enhancing resident decision making → `F-Tag 841`
- [P0468] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Medical director must participate meaningfully in Quality Assessment and Assurance (QAA) committee or assign/oversee designee → `F-Tag 841`
- [P0469] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Medical director must collaborate with facility to develop/implement infection control surveillance policies/procedures → `F-Tag 841`
- [P0470] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Medical director must help develop systems to monitor health care practitioner performance including mechanisms for communicating/resolving issues → `F-Tag 841`
- [P0471] **PERMISO** — If medical director is also attending physician, must have process to address concerns with individual's performance as physician → `F-Tag 841`
- [P0472] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Medical director must respond to surveyors on survey-related issues and help facility analyze deficiencies/identify areas for improvement → `F-Tag 841`
