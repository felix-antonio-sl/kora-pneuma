# Crónicas del Crepúsculo Geriátrico - Parte 14

## Conclusion

- [P1047] **HECHO** — HF 5-year mortality: 74% with normal LVEF, 92% with reduced LVEF (repeated for emphasis in conclusion)
- [P1048] **HECHO** — HTN treatment in >80 yr results in significant reduction in stroke and all-cause mortality within 1-2 yr

## Introduction

- [P1049] **HECHO** — National average readmission rate from NF/SNF ≈18% for FY 2019 per CMS
- [P1050] **REGLA** — Affordable Care Act established Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program (HRRP) starting October 1, 2012
- [P1051] **DEFINICIÓN** — HRRP goal: encourage hospitals improve communication/care coordination to reduce avoidable readmissions
- [P1052] **DEFINICIÓN** — Excess Readmission Ratio (ERR) = ratio of predicted-to-expected readmission rates; measures hospital performance vs regional/national rates
- [P1053] **HECHO** — CMS evaluates 6 diagnoses via ERR: heart failure, acute MI, COPD, pneumonia, CABG surgery, elective primary total hip/knee arthroplasty
- [P1054] **REGLA** — HRRP reports 30-day risk-standardized unplanned readmission rate including readmissions to same or another acute care hospital for any reason
- [P1055] **REGLA** — Protecting Access to Medicare Act 2014 mandated penalties for NF/SNF starting October 1, 2018 for excessive readmissions → `HRRP`
- [P1056] **HECHO** — Patient Driven Payment Model (PDPM) replaced fee-for-service in FY 2020
- [P1057] **HECHO** — CMS provided NF/SNF quarterly readmission reports starting October 2016
- [P1058] **HECHO** — FY 2019: facilities could obtain 1.6% Medicare Part A payment bonus or 2% payment reduction based on readmission data
- [P1059] **HECHO** — 3% of SNFs received maximum bonus 1.6%; ≈20% of SNFs got maximum cut 2%
- [P1060] **HECHO** — Adverse hospital outcomes for frail elders include: distress/discomfort, delirium (environment change), polypharmacy/drug errors in transitions, falls/fractures, incontinence/improper catheter use, HAI, weight loss/poor nutrition, immobility/de-conditioning/pressure wounds (Ouslander 2016)

## Transitions of Care

- [P1061] **HECHO** — Care transitions primarily involve 3 systems: NF/SNF, hospital, primary care in community
- [P1062] **HECHO** — Most transitions: hospital→NF/SNF, NF/SNF→hospital, NF/SNF→community
- [P1063] **REQUISITO** — Successful transfers require: (1) patient-centered/family-oriented care, (2) effective communication between all care systems
- [P1064] **OBLIGACIÓN** — Imperative to review plan of care with healthcare proxy, POA, and/or family caregivers as well as patient
- [P1065] **OBLIGACIÓN** — During care transition, goals of care must be reviewed with patient (if capacity) or proxy/surrogate decision maker
- [P1066] **HECHO** — Care transitions = opportune time to clarify wishes re: future hospitalizations, ER visits, NF/SNF care
- [P1067] **REGLA** — Goals of care should include discussions on CPR, DNR, and if appropriate "do not transfer to ER" / "do not admit to hospital"
- [P1068] **DEFINICIÓN** — Geriatrics 4M model (2017, IHI + AHA): Mind, Mobility, Medications, Matters Most
- [P1069] **HECHO** — Mind = dementia/delirium evaluation; Mobility = fall prevention/evaluation; Medications = polypharmacy review/de-prescribing; Matters Most = advance care planning/serious illness conversation
- [P1070] **HECHO** — Some organizations added 5th M: multi-morbidity (multi-complexity) as additional factor in reducing avoidable hospitalizations

## Effective Communication

- [P1071] **REQUISITO** — Discharge summary should be sent with patient at time of discharge + copy to PCP
- [P1072] **REQUISITO** — Discharge summary must include: updated medication list + reasons for changes, discharge instructions, conditions to monitor, contact person at discharging facility, pertinent consultations/ER visits/follow-up visits, copies of advance directives and goals of care including DNR status
- [P1073] **REQUISITO** — Follow-up phone call to discharged patients/caregivers should discuss: medication issues (reconciliation), planned follow-up visits, follow-up lab work/testing
- [P1074] **REQUISITO** — To reduce avoidable admissions: (1) practitioners must acquire/maintain excellent geriatric competencies, (2) NF/SNF must ensure timely follow-up upon admission or acute change

## PCP Fax Transitional Care Summary: SNF to Home

- [P1075] **HECHO** — NF discharge summaries frequently not completed by day of discharge → delay in information transfer to PCP
- [P1076] **OBLIGACIÓN** — PCP Fax Summary should be completed and faxed on day of discharge to receiving PCP
- [P1077] **RESTRICCIÓN** — PCP Fax should not be faxed before day of transfer (may be incomplete) nor after (may be forgotten)
- [P1078] **REGLA** — PCP Fax does not require signature as it would delay completion
- [P1079] **RESTRICCIÓN** — Do not give PCP Fax copy to patient in lieu of faxing; PCP may not receive it timely

## Accountable Care Organizations (ACO)

- [P1080] **DEFINICIÓN** — CMS defines ACOs as groups of doctors/hospitals/providers who voluntarily coordinate high-quality care to Medicare patients
- [P1081] **REGLA** — Medicare incentivizes ACOs by sharing cost savings while placing ACO at risk for financial losses if quality standards not met
- [P1082] **HECHO** — 3 diagnoses consistently common reasons for hospitalization from NF: septicemia, CHF, pneumonia (per Medicare/AHRQ data)
- [P1083] **HECHO** — 60-75% of hospital admissions for septicemia/CHF/pneumonia from NF/SNF are potentially avoidable
- [P1084] **HECHO** — ACOs can refer frail adults with acute illness directly to NF/SNF rather than hospital admission
- [P1085] **HECHO** — INTERACT II (Ouslander et al.) when implemented in nursing homes demonstrated improved quality of care and reduction of avoidable hospitalizations

## Acute Changes in Condition

- [P1086] **REQUISITO** — Physicians/NPs/PAs must be available to assess patients within 24-48h when acute change in condition occurs
- [P1087] **HECHO** — Common acute changes requiring licensed staff knowledge: change in mental status, chest pain, CHF, dehydration, acute aggressive behavior, fever of unknown origin, UTI/asymptomatic bacteriuria, pneumonia

## Change in Mental Status

- [P1088] **HECHO** — Frequent causes of acute mental status change in frail older adults: medication side effects, new-onset infections, cognitive decline progression, pain
- [P1089] **HECHO** — Prevalence of memory loss in NF patients: 50-80%
- [P1090] **REQUISITO** — Critical to obtain baseline cognitive assessment within first few days after admission to NF or assisted living
- [P1091] **REGLA** — When recently admitted patient diagnosed with delirium/depression, cognition baseline should be reevaluated after resolution of acute change

## Chest Pain

- [P1092] **TENSIÓN** — Chest pain is emergency but calling 911 may not be best response depending on patient goals of care
- [P1093] **REGLA** — For patients with "comfort" goal of care: treat chest pain with aspirin, SL nitroglycerin, SL morphine sulfate as appropriate (especially if on hospice)

## Congestive Heart Failure (CHF)

- [P1094] **HECHO** — CHF is one of most common diagnoses for NF→hospital transfers; many are avoidable
- [P1095] **OBLIGACIÓN** — During monthly/bimonthly visits, residents' weights should be reviewed
- [P1096] **REGLA** — Except for rare situations (e.g., ruptured heart valve), PCPs should treat CHF at facility and avoid hospitalization unless refractory pulmonary edema develops
